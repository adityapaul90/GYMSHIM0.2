The problem

Five ways a gym quietly loses money

Every one of these is normal. None of them should be.

00 📵

Leads go cold

As your gym business grows, you find it challenging to maintain records. The traditional way of maintaining records in books and excel sheets. Several times crucial information slips through the cracks causing heavy business losses

00 👻

Attendance is invisible

Even if you are very meticulous in maintaining records, how will you use the data that you have maintained so painstakingly? Just to come up with some basic statistics, you will have to refer to numerous sheets of data.

00 ⏳

Renewals sneak past

How many active members do you have currently? How many members’ renewal is coming up? How many members have paid in part, when is their next payment due? Can’t answer without looking up sheets of data?

00 📊

Reporting eats a weekend

Attendance records, financial records, members information, things-to-do lists, packages & offers information! If you are wasting your precious time on keeping track of all of these, when will you find time to grow your business?

00 🧩

Nothing talks to anything

Front desk, trainers, and billing all run on separate tools — so the same piece of information gets typed in three times, differently, three times.
